Help for Scora One users.

Find quick answers about pricing, privacy, offline use, purchases, and how to report an issue.

FAQ

Is Scora One free?

Yes. Scora One is available with optional Premium features.

Are my data sent over the Internet?

No. Scora One is designed to work locally, and no personal data is sent by default.

Can I restore my purchases?

Yes. Purchases can be restored through the App Store restore flow.

Can I use Scora One without a connection?

Yes. Scora One can be used offline for scoring, templates, and saved boards.

How do I report a problem?

Send a message to support@example.com with your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.
