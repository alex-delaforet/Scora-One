Support

Help for Scora One users.

Find quick answers about pricing, privacy, offline use, purchases, iCloud synchronization, and how to report an issue.

FAQ

Is Scora One free?

Yes. Scora One is available with optional Premium features.

Are my data sent over the Internet?

Scora One does not send your personal data to servers operated by the developer. The app is designed to work locally.

If you choose to use the iCloud synchronization feature, supported app data is sent to Apple's iCloud services so it can be synchronized between your Apple devices.

How does iCloud synchronization work?

iCloud synchronization is optional and is started manually by using the synchronization feature in Scora One.

Scora One does not automatically synchronize your data with iCloud. When you choose to synchronize, supported app data is stored through Apple's iCloud services and can be restored or synchronized on your other Apple devices.

Can I restore my purchases?

Yes. Purchases can be restored through the App Store restore flow.

Can I use Scora One without a connection?

Yes. Scora One can be used offline for scoring, templates, and saved boards. iCloud synchronization requires an Internet connection.

How do I report a problem?

Send a message to dev.lepage@orange.fr with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of the issue.
