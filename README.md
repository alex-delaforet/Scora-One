# Scora-One
Scora One is a mobile app to follow scores during matches, tournaments and many more.


Scora One turns your iPhone or iPad into a customizable scoreboard for matches, board games and games with friends.

Play. Scora One keeps score.

Start a game in seconds and keep scores, counters and timers right where you need them.

• Create and customize your games
• Easily track scores and counters
• Use built-in timers
• Automatically detect the winner
• Automatically save your games
• Keep track of your game history
• Create your own templates
• Customize your scoreboard
• Easily share your results
• Enjoy animations and haptic feedback

Your games. Your templates.

Set up the rules and settings for your favorite games once, then start a new game in seconds.

Make it yours.

Choose your theme and customize your experience to create a scoreboard that fits the way you play.

Go further with Premium.

Scora One Premium unlocks additional customization and flexibility:

• Higher template limits
• Advanced themes and customization
• Template import and export
• Additional Premium features like timeouts in match mode and much more.

No ads.

Scora One contains no advertising.

Whether you're playing a board game at home or keeping score during a match with friends, Scora One keeps the one thing that matters most right where you need it: the score.
