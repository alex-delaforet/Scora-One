Features

A premium scoreboard for every format.

Scora One brings together counters, match boards, tournament tables, templates, and history in a clean interface optimized for iPhone and iPad.

Interface

Premium by design.

Scora One is built around clarity, with rich materials, smooth transitions, and extremely readable layouts. The interface keeps the score front and center so players and spectators understand the game state immediately.

  • Clean structure and generous spacing
  • Modern materials and high contrast surfaces
  • Large score typography built for distance

The three modes

Each mode is focused on a real scoring need, with controls that stay direct and readable.

08

Counter

Use Counter for simple games, quick score tracking, and any moment where you only need plus and minus controls.

1:25

Match

Use Match for team play with a timer, sets, team names, and a display that keeps the match state visible.

16

Tournament

Use Tournament for automatic rankings, unlimited players, and point management across a full event.

C

Customization

Adjust colors, names, icons, teams, players, and templates to match the game you are running.

H

History

All games remain available, so previous boards and score states can be found again when needed.

S

Automatic saving

Templates are saved automatically, making repeated events and favorite boards faster to launch.

A

Accessibility

Large text, very strong readability, dark mode, and high contrast colors make Scora One easy to read during play.

Performance

Fast, fluid, and optimized.

Scora One is designed to feel immediate on iPhone and iPad, with lightweight screens, fast setup, and smooth interactions for repeated use during games and events.

  • Fast scoreboard creation
  • Fluid controls during live scoring
  • Optimized layouts for iPhone and iPad
