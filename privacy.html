Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Scora One is developed by Alexandre Lepage. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Scora One application.

Data Collection

Scora One does not collect personal data.

The app does not operate its own servers, databases, advertising systems, or third-party tracking services. Data entered or created while using Scora One is used solely to provide the app's features.

Data Stored by Scora One

Scora One does not store personal data on servers operated by the developer.

Information such as scoreboards, templates, history, and preferences may be stored locally on your device as part of the normal operation of the application.

Scora One does not have access to this locally stored information.

iCloud

Scora One may use Apple's iCloud services to synchronize certain app data between your Apple devices.

This iCloud data is stored and managed by Apple through your iCloud account. It is not stored on servers operated by the developer, and Scora One does not have access to the contents of your iCloud data.

The handling of iCloud data is subject to Apple's privacy practices and your iCloud settings.

In-App Purchases

Scora One may offer optional Premium features through Apple's StoreKit.

Payments, billing information, purchase processing, refunds, and related transaction information are handled by Apple. Scora One does not receive or store your payment or credit card information.

Analytics

Scora One does not use third-party analytics or tracking services.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated information related to the app through Apple's developer and App Store services, such as downloads, sales, and crash information. This information is provided and handled by Apple according to Apple's policies and available user settings.

Scora One does not use this information to identify or track individual users.

Data Sharing

Scora One does not sell, rent, or share personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

The developer does not have access to personal data stored locally on your device or in your iCloud account.

Data Deletion

Because Scora One does not store personal data on servers operated by the developer, there is no developer-side account or database to delete.

You can delete locally stored Scora One data by deleting the app or using the data management options provided by the app, where available.

Data stored through iCloud is managed through Apple's iCloud services and your Apple account settings.

Your Rights

If you are located in the European Economic Area or another jurisdiction providing data protection rights, you may have rights regarding your personal data.

Since Scora One does not collect or store personal data on the developer's servers, most such requests can generally be handled through your device, app settings, or Apple account settings.

For any privacy-related question concerning Scora One, you can contact the developer using the contact information below.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Scora One or applicable requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page.
