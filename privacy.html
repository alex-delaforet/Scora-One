Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Scora One is developed by Alexandre Lepage. This policy explains what information is collected, stored, and used when you use the Scora One application.

Data Collected

By default, Scora One does not collect personal data. The application only uses the information needed to operate scoreboards, templates, history, preferences, and purchase status.

In-App Purchases

Scora One may offer optional Premium features through Apple StoreKit. Purchase processing, payment information, invoices, refunds, and purchase history are handled by Apple according to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Data Stored

Scora One may store templates, scoreboard history, and preferences. This information is stored on your device unless a future version clearly states otherwise.

Analytics

Scora One does not use third-party tracking. Apple may provide aggregated App Store analytics to the developer, such as downloads, purchases, and crash information, according to Apple's developer tools and user settings.

Data Sharing

Scora One does not sell personal data. Data stored locally in the app is not shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Deletion

You can delete local Scora One data by removing the application from your device or by using any data deletion options available in the app settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@example.com.
